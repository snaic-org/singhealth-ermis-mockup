Screen Element Description

  1. Notifications: In-app notifications for risk monitoring, KRI review dates, outstanding actions and approvals, also sent via email.
  2. Quick actions: Shortcuts to start a new review, open the risk register or search similar risks. AI-assisted creation (add-on) starts a review through the AI chatbot, which asks questions and fills in the form from the answers.
  3. Filters: A single fixed filter (the user's own department). Changing filters and dashboard items is an add-on.
  4. Risk domain profile: Shows how risks are spread across risk domains, by rating and control adequacy.
  5. Residual heat map: Shows residual risks by impact and likelihood.
  6. Control effectiveness: Shows risks by control effectiveness, highlighting under-controlled risks.
  7. Priority residual risks: Highest-rated risks with owners and control adequacy.
  8. Reviews in progress: Reviews from draft through approval to endorsed.

Assumption

  • Heat Map: The heat map shows residual risk (after current controls).
