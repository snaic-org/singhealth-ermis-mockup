System Functions & Flow

Screen Element Description

  1. Dashboard: Overview of risks, the review queue and notifications, with shortcuts to start a review or search.
  2. Review Workflow: Guides the user through the review, then compiles the KRM plan and routes it for approval, with return for revision. Drafts can be saved and resumed.
  3. Knowledge Base & Search: Stores past reviews and ORS guidelines as a knowledge base. AI uses it to draft risk statements, controls, additional controls and KRIs in the review, and to find similar risks by meaning in Risk Search.
  4. Data: Data Import loads past Excel workbooks into the risk register and the knowledge base. The Risk Register stores endorsed reviews and exports on demand.
  5. Notifications: In-app and email reminders for risk monitoring, KRI review dates, outstanding actions and approvals.

Assumption

  • Endorsement: Endorsement is online: the HOD approves or returns the plan in the system.
  • Domains: Risks are classified under the 12 SingHealth risk domains.
