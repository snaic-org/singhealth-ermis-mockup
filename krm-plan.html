Screen Element Description

  1. KRM plan: Compiled from the review: header details, objectives and the table of risks, controls and indicators.
  2. Approval workflow: The plan is submitted, then endorsed online by the HOD or returned with comments. A returned plan can be revised and resubmitted.
  3. Endorsed & registered: An endorsed plan is added to the risk register and the coordinator is notified.

Assumption

  • Template: One standard KRM template. Individual templates are not included.
  • Endorsement: Endorsement is recorded online with the HOD name and date.
  • Roles: The HOD has an approver role and sees plans awaiting approval on the dashboard.
