Screen Element Description

  1. Scope: Departments, risk domain and review cycles to analyse.
  2. Analyses: Shows trends and patterns across departments. AI groups similar risk statements into clusters and finds controls used in one department but missing in another. Rating trajectory and risk domain breakdown are counted from the data.
  3. Summary report: Produces a summary report for export to PDF. AI drafts the summary of the main trends, clusters and gaps, and the user edits it.

Assumption

  • Data: Results depend on the amount and quality of the loaded history.
