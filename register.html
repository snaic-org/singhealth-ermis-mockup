Screen Element Description

  1. Filters: Department, objective, risk domain, rating, owner and review cycle.
  2. Registered risks: Endorsed reviews are added automatically, with no re-typing.
  3. Export register: On demand, by department, review cycle or full dataset.
  4. Copy to a new review: Makes a copy of an existing review as a new draft.

Assumption

  • Risk Domains: All 12 domains are labels used to classify and filter risks.
