SingHealth ERMIS Mockup

Schematic mockups designed to help users visualise the basic system and evaluate optional add-on features to determine project scope and budget. The Overall Flow Diagram shows the envisioned system: its screens and functions, and how they connect.

Baseline Envisioned System Screens

Screen ID Screen Name Function Scope Note
WF-01 Dashboard Department overview: heat map, control adequacy, top risks, and reviews in progress. 4 standard views
WF-02 Review Start Page Guided review: goal, objectives, risks, controls, and KRM plan. 1 standard workflow
WF-03 KRM Plan & Endorsement Compiles the standard KRM plan and routes it for online endorsement. 1 standard template
WF-04 Department Risk Register Live register of endorsed reviews. Filter, copy, or export. 12 risk domains
WF-05 Risk Search Finds similar past risks, with their controls and indicators. Searches loaded reviews
WF-06 Data Import Uploads and migrates existing Excel workbooks into the new system. Excel v3.1 only

Add-On Modules

Add-on Module Name Function Scope Note
WF-A1 Enhanced Interface Configuration and personalisation, an AI chatbot guide, indicator management and conditional workflow. Config, personalisation, chatbot
WF-A2 AI Trend Analytics & Reporting Trends, risk clustering and control gap comparison across departments, with an AI summary report. 4 analyses, 1 report
WF-A3 Horizon Scanning Proof-of-concept scan of one approved external source for emerging risks, compared with the register. 1 source, trial only
