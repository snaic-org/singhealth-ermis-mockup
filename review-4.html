Screen Element Description

  1. Reference: Opens the reference panel.
  2. Search similar risks: Opens Risk Search to find similar past risks and copy their additional controls into this review.
  3. Current posture: Residual rating and adequacy from Step 3.
  4. Additional controls & action plan: Captures additional controls, the change in effectiveness, target date and action owner. AI drafts additional controls from those used for similar risks in past reviews and ORS guidelines, with the source shown. The user accepts or edits the draft. The system calculates the projected rating.

Assumption

  • Optional Controls: As in ERM Tool v3.1, additional controls are optional.
