Screen Element Description

  1. Search: Finds past risks from a plain-language question or description. AI matches it by meaning, so risks worded differently are still found.
  2. Filters: Risk domain, department, rating and cycle.
  3. Results: Lists similar past risks with their controls and indicators, which can be copied into a review. AI ranks them by similarity, most similar first, with a similarity score.
  4. Export results: Exports the current search results on demand.

Assumption

  • Data: Search covers the historical reviews loaded for the pilot.
