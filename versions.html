Version History

Record of mockup versions and what changed in each. Current version: v1.1.

Version Date Status What changed Screens affected
v1.1 16 Oct 2026 Current
  • Additional controls: the change in effectiveness has 13 options (from maximum effectiveness to control removed). Control adequacy keeps its 4 classes; the rating levels are separate.
  • Risk rating engine: rule-based, following the existing Excel formulas and 5-level scale. AI does not calculate ratings.
  • Automated notifications: in-app notifications and email reminders for risk monitoring, KRI review dates and outstanding actions, in the baseline.
  • Access: 3 access tiers and 6 reporting views replaced by simple user, approver (HOD) and admin roles, now in the baseline.
  • AI assistance: AI drafts risk statements, current controls, additional controls and KRIs inside the review form on Steps 2 to 5, with the source shown.
  • Dashboard: "AI-assisted creation" quick action added as an add-on (WF-A1).
  • Dashboard: notifications moved to the top; quick actions separated; one fixed filter (own department) in the baseline; changing filters and dashboard items is an add-on (WF-A1).
  • References: opened from a Reference button on each review step.
  • Enhanced Interface add-on: configuration, personalisation and an AI chatbot guide for filling in reviews.
  • Approval workflow and draft saving: route for approval, save drafts, resume, and revise after rejection, in the baseline.
  • Reporting and export: no automatic quarterly reports; search, view and export on demand.
  • Control Self-Assessment: removed from the POC scope.
  • Horizon Scanning: stays an optional add-on, outside the baseline.
  • KRM plan: one standard template; reviews and endorsements completed online.
  • Step 1: healthcare institution field added.
  • Register: from up to 3 registers to all 12 risk domains, renamed "risk domain" (classification labels).
 Dashboard, Review Steps 1–5, KRM Plan, Register, Enhanced Interface, Horizon Scanning; Self-Assessment removed
v1.0 15 Oct 2026 Initial
  • Initial scoping mockup: baseline screens and add-ons WF-A1 to WF-A3.
  • Baseline: 5-step review with 6 reference sets, AI drafting, rule-based ratings, KRM plan with paper endorsement, register, search, self-assessment, data ingestion and a 4-view dashboard.
