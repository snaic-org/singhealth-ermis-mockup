WF-02 · Review Start Page

Guided 5-step review that replaces the Excel v3.1 workbook.

1Review steps

Step Function & Data Captured Rating / Logic Action
Step 1: Description & Goal Captures department, project location, healthcare institution and strategic goal. Qualitative context Open Step 1 →
Step 2: Objectives & Risks Captures operational objectives and risk statements in the standard format, with a risk domain from the 12 options. Inherent rating (calculated as in ERM Tool v3.1) Open Step 2 →
Step 3: Current Controls Records existing safeguards and an overall control effectiveness rating (7-level scale). Residual Risk & Adequacy (RULES) Open Step 3 →
Step 4: Addition / Change to Controls Records additional treatment actions with deadlines and action owners. Projected Residual Rating (RULES) Open Step 4 →
Step 5: Monitoring Records Key Risk Indicators (KRIs) with thresholds and monitoring leads. Monitoring framework Open Step 5 →
KRM Plan Endorsement Compile the standard KRM plan and route it to the HOD for online endorsement. Online approval Open KRM Plan →
Risk Register Endorsed plans are added to the department risk register, where they can be filtered, exported or copied into a new review. Added on endorsement Open Risk Register →

Screen Element Description

  1. Review steps: The five steps, the KRM plan and the risk register, each with a link to its page.

Assumption

  • Sequence: Steps follow the ERM Tool v3.1 order (1 → 2 → 3 → 4 → 5).
