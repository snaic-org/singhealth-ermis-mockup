Screen Element Description

  1. Reference: Opens the reference panel.
  2. Search similar risks: Opens Risk Search to find similar past risks and copy their controls into this review.
  3. Risk summary: Read-only summary of the risk from Step 2.
  4. Current controls & effectiveness: Captures current controls and their effectiveness. AI drafts current controls from those used for similar risks in past reviews and ORS guidelines, with the source shown. The user accepts or edits the draft. The system calculates the residual rating and control adequacy.

Assumption

  • Rating: One overall effectiveness rating per risk.
