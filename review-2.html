Screen Element Description

  1. Reference: Opens the reference panel.
  2. Search similar risks: Opens Risk Search to find similar past risks and copy their wording into this review.
  3. Operational objectives: Captures the operational objectives of the review.
  4. Risk formulation & inherent rating: Captures each risk: risk statement, consequences, risk domain, impact, likelihood and risk owner. AI drafts the risk statement from the objective and details entered, based on past reviews and ORS guidelines, with the source shown. The user accepts or edits the draft.

Assumption

  • Risk Domain: Each risk has one risk domain, chosen from the 12 SingHealth risk domains, as in ERM Tool v3.1.
